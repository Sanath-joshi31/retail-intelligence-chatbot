# Shipping and Fulfillment Operations Guidelines

## 1. Shipping Methods & Speed
* **Standard Ground Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days. Free for loyalty members on orders over $50; flat rate $5.99 for other orders.
* **Expedited 2-Day Air**: Guaranteed delivery within 2 business days if placed before 2:00 PM EST. Flat rate $14.99.
* **Next-Day Priority**: Next business day delivery by 10:30 AM or end of day. Flat rate $24.99.
* **White Glove Freight Delivery**: Required for heavy appliances and oversized exercise equipment over 70 lbs. Includes room of choice delivery, unboxing, and debris removal for $99.

## 2. Order Cutoff & Fulfillment SLAs
* Standard warehouse order processing time: within **24 hours** from order authorization.
* Same-day dispatch cutoff: Orders placed before **2:00 PM EST** Monday through Friday ship same day.
* Weekend orders: Processed and dispatched on the following Monday morning.

## 3. Damaged or Lost Shipments
* Damaged in transit shipments must be reported within **48 hours** of carrier delivery scan with photographic proof of carton and item damage.
* Courier loss claims are processed with instant reshipment after 72 hours of carrier stalled scan status.
