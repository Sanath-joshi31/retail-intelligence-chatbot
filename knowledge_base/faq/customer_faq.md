# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What is your price matching policy?
**A:** We match current advertised prices from authorized major retailers (Best Buy, Amazon direct, Target, Walmart) for identical in-stock items. Price matches must be requested at the time of sale with valid competitor link or circular proof.

### Q: Can I buy online and pick up in store (BOPIS)?
**A:** Yes. In-store pickup orders are typically prepared and ready within **2 hours** of placement during normal store operating hours. Customers have 7 days to pick up their order before it is cancelled and refunded.

### Q: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, cash, store gift cards, and approved 0% APR installment financing.

### Q: How do loyalty points work?
**A:** Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Every 100 points converts to a $5 reward certificate redeemable across all products in store or online. Points do not expire as long as account shows purchase activity within 12 months.

### Q: How do I track my order delivery?
**A:** Once your order ships, a tracking number and courier tracking link is emailed and visible under the order history tab in your account dashboard.
