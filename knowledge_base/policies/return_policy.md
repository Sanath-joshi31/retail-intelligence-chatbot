# Retail Return and Refund Policy

## 1. General Return Guidelines
Customers may return most new, unopened items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging, including all manuals, accessories, and warranty cards. Proof of purchase (original receipt or digital invoice) is required for all returns.

## 2. Electronics Category Policy
* **Return Window**: All electronics (including smartphones, laptops, tablets, smartwatches, and audio equipment) must be returned within **14 days** of purchase or delivery date.
* **Condition Requirements**:
  * Must be in original manufacturer packaging with UPC barcode intact.
  * All factory reset procedures must be performed by the customer before return. Accounts (such as iCloud, Google Account, Microsoft Account) must be fully signed out and unlinked.
  * Opened items that show signs of misuse, water damage, or physical impact are ineligible for return.
* **Restocking Fee**: A **15% restocking fee** applies to opened electronics in working condition if returned without packaging defects.
* **Defective Products**: Defective electronics can be exchanged for an identical replacement within **30 days** or serviced under the manufacturer warranty.

## 3. Clothing and Apparel Policy
* **Return Window**: Clothing, footwear, and accessories have a **45-day** return window.
* **Condition Requirements**: Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original retail tags attached.
* **Exceptions**: Underwear, swimwear without protective liners, and clearance final-sale items cannot be returned.

## 4. Refund Processing
* Refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within **3 to 5 business days** following inspection at the warehouse.
* Cash purchases over $100 will be refunded via company check or electronic bank transfer.
* Shipping and handling charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a merchant fulfillment error.
