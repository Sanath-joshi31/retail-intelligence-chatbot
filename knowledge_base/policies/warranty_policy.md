# Comprehensive Product Warranty Policy

## 1. Standard Limited Warranty Coverage
All brand-new retail products sold through our stores and online platform come with a complimentary manufacturer-backed warranty:
* **Consumer Electronics & Computers**: 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty covering manufacturing defects, component failure, and internal hardware faults under normal usage conditions.
* **Home & Kitchen Appliances**: 2-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty on motor assemblies, heating elements, and electronic controls.
* **Sports & Fitness Gear**: 1-Year Structural Frame & 90-Day Moving Parts Warranty.
* **Apparel & Footwear**: 90-Day Workmanship Warranty covering seam separation, zipper failures, and fabric flaws.

## 2. Extended Warranty Options ("RetailCare Plus")
Customers may purchase 2-year or 3-year RetailCare Plus coverage at checkout:
* Covers accidental damage from handling (drops, spills, cracked screens).
* Includes unlimited battery replacements if capacity degrades below 80%.
* Offers no-deductible priority in-store repair or immediate equivalent replacement if turnaround exceeds 5 business days.

## 3. Exclusions
The following conditions are not covered under standard or extended warranty:
* Unauthorized third-party modifications or non-certified repair attempts.
* Intentional physical abuse, liquid submersion exceeding IP rating specs, or cosmetic scratches from normal wear and tear.
* Loss, theft, or natural disaster damages.

## 4. Warranty Claim Procedure
1. Customer presents the original receipt or order number and serial number.
2. Store technician runs hardware diagnostics (average turnaround 15-30 minutes).
3. If defect is confirmed, store provides instant replacement (if in stock) or dispatches unit to authorized repair depot.
