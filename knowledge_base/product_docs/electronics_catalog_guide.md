# Electronics Product Catalog and Compatibility Guide

## 1. Flagship Smartphones
* **Apple iPhone 15 / 15 Pro**: USB-C port, Dynamic Island, A16/A17 Pro bionic chips. Supports MagSafe wireless charging up to 15W and Qi2 standard. Requires 20W or higher power adapter for fast charging (50% in 30 mins).
* **Samsung Galaxy S24 / S24 Ultra**: Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, integrated S-Pen (Ultra model), 45W Super Fast Charging 2.0. Requires 5A rated USB-C E-marker cable for full charging velocity.

## 2. Laptops and Portable Computing
* **MacBook Air / Pro (M3 series)**: Native Apple Silicon architecture. Liquid Retina XDR displays, MagSafe 3 power port, Thunderbolt 4 / USB 4 connectivity. Battery health management active by default.
* **Dell XPS & ThinkPad Laptops**: Intel Core Ultra / AMD Ryzen processors with dedicated NPU for local AI tasks. Power Delivery (PD 3.0) via Type-C charging.

## 3. Product Handling and Storage Guidelines
* Store all lithium-ion battery devices at temperatures between **15°C and 25°C** (59°F to 77°F) with optimal storage charge of 50%.
* Never expose boxed inventory to direct moisture or humidity above 75%.
